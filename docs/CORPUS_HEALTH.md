## What the numbers say

**The archive is clean, shallow and lopsided.**

- **Clean**: 0 exact duplicates (verified over all 1.33M docs), 0.0% of sampled docs
  contain a URL/email/phone, 85.3% mean Tamil-script share with only 1.0% of docs
  below 70%.
- **Shallow**: the median document is **302 characters** and **32% are under 200**.
  The culprit is IndicCorp (median 237 chars); FineWeb-2 (median 1,717) and Wikipedia
  (961) are the only long-form sources. Pretraining sees fewer document boundaries
  per token than the mixture suggests, and short docs mean less coherent context per
  packed window.
- **Lopsided**: IndicCorp is **72% of tokens** and already exceeds its own register
  target, while **education and legal/government are at zero** — there is no source
  for them at all.

### One real defect: 7.3% near-duplicates

Exact dedup works; near-duplicate removal during ingestion does not survive contact
with reality. Ingestion used `num_perm=16, bands=4` (~0.71 threshold) and compared
new documents against each other plus *exact hashes* of the archive, never signatures
of existing shards. Measured now at 16 perms / 4 bands, **7.3% of sampled documents
share an LSH band** with an earlier document, concentrated in:

| source | near-dup share of its sample |
|---|---|
| `fineweb-2-ta` | **24.7%** |
| `wikipedia-ta` | **10.9%** |
| `ai4bharat-indiccorp-v2` | 3.3% |
| local Sangam texts (many tiny files) | 12–50% |

FineWeb-2 at ~25% is the expected web-crawl signature (syndicated copies, mirrors).
The fix is cheap and already available: `scripts/run_dedup.py --num-perm 128` over
the archive, or seed the ingest's LSH with existing shard signatures instead of only
exact hashes. Budget ~7% of the corpus in lost tokens — which is the honest reason
not to grow the corpus before deduplicating it.

### A false alarm worth knowing about

`phase2_validate.py` reports ~0.9% PII; a stricter scan finds **0.0%**. Its phone
pattern matches any 10–11 digit run, so years, account numbers and codes count as
telephone numbers. The archive is not leaking PII — the validator is over-reporting,
and that should be fixed before anyone uses its PII number as a gate.

## How much more, and of what

Holding 328.4M tokens, the compute-optimal question has a sharp answer:

| model | tokens for ~20 tok/param | have | **more needed** |
|---|---|---|---|
| 16M (`adhan-nano`) | 320M | 328M | **0 — done** |
| 50M | 1.0B | 328M | **672M** |
| 100M | 2.0B | 328M | **1.67B** |
| 300M | 6.0B | 328M | **5.67B** |

So: **for the nano tier the corpus is sufficient today**; every tier above it is a
data project, not a hyperparameter project.

Against register targets the gap is **181.8M tokens**, and it is not spread evenly:

| priority | register | gap | note |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | consumer web (FineWeb-2) | **81.8M** | the original 400k-doc plan was OOM-killed at 157k; resuming is the cheapest large win (~2 h streaming) |
| 2 | education (SCERT/textbooks) | **60.0M** | **no source exists yet** — needs a new ingest path (PDF → text), highest value per token for a curriculum product |
| 3 | legal / government | **30.0M** | also no source; PDF/gazette scraping |
| 4 | encyclopaedic (Wikipedia) | **10.0M** | Tamil Wikipedia is near its practical limit (~140k articles) |

**Order of operations, if the goal is a better model rather than a bigger number:**

1. **Deduplicate the 7.3%** (`run_dedup.py --num-perm 128`) — the archive shrinks,
   and every later token is worth more. Do this *before* buying more data.
2. **Resume FineWeb-2 to 400k docs** (~82M tokens, ~2 h) — the register the model is
   thinnest on, with a per-document `language_score` to keep quality up.
3. **Only then** chase education/legal, which need new extraction machinery and
   careful licensing review.

Skip step 1 and the next 180M tokens will inherit the same 7% duplication.
